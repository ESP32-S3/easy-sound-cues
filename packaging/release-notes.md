## Which file do I download?

| Your computer | Download this file |
| --- | --- |
| **Windows** 10 or 11 | **Easy-Sound-Cues-Windows.exe** |
| **Mac** (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 10.13 or newer) | **Easy-Sound-Cues-Mac.dmg** |
| **Linux** (64-bit) | **Easy-Sound-Cues-Linux.tar.gz** |

The other files (`easy-sound-cues-mac`, `easy-sound-cues-linux-x64`) are for the app's automatic updates. You don't need them.

The first time you open the app, your computer may warn you about it, because it's free and isn't "signed" with a paid certificate. The [instructions](https://github.com/ESP32-S3/easy-sound-cues#opening-it-for-the-first-time) show the one click that gets past each warning.

Already have the app? It updates itself: look for the green **Update now** button at the top of the window.
